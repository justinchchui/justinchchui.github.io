Stable Quantum Orbits

Electrons can only orbit in discrete, non-radiating "stationary states". Light is absorbed or emitted ONLY when an electron jumps between orbit levels (ΔE = hν).

Challenge 3: Why was Rutherford's model rejected?

Press 🌀 Start Classical Radiation Loss. Classical physics mandates that any accelerating charged particle continuously radiates energy. This would produce a continuous spectrum (rainbow of all wavelengths) rather than sharp spectral lines!
