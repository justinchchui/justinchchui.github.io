1. Sinusoidal Standing Waves: Microwave radiation reflects off the metallic interior cavity walls, creating a standing wave pattern: $E(x, t) = E_0 \sin(k x) \cos(\omega t)$.

2. Antinodes vs. Nodes: At wave Antinodes ($|\sin(kx)| = 1$), the electric field oscillates at maximum amplitude, exerting intense torque $\vec{\tau} = \vec{p} \times \vec{E}$ on polar water molecules. At wave Nodes ($\sin(kx) = 0$), $E = 0$, so zero dielectric heating occurs!

3. Collisional Thermal Transfer: Rotating water molecules violently collide with neighboring non-polar food molecules, converting rotational friction into thermal lattice vibrations.

💡 Key Takeaway: When the Turntable is OFF, food at antinodes burns while food at nodes stays ice cold. Turning the Turntable ON spins the turntable along its axis, rotating food through high and low field zones to produce an evenly cooked meal!
