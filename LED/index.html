Particle Legend
  • Free Electron ($e^-$): Mobile negative charge
  • Hole ($h^+$): Absence of electron (mobile positive)
  • + Donor Ion ($P^+$): Fixed positive charge
  • - Acceptor Ion ($B^-$): Fixed negative charge
  • Photon: Energy released during recombination
HKDSE Exam Key Takeaways:

• Semiconductors: Silicon has 4 valence electrons forming covalent bonds. Doping with Group V (Phosphorus) introduces free electrons (N-type). Doping with Group III (Boron) introduces holes (P-type).

• P-N Junction & Depletion: When joined, electrons & holes diffuse across the interface and recombine. This leaves behind uncompensated immobile ions ($P^+$ on N-side, $B^-$ on P-side), establishing an internal electric field that halts further diffusion.

• LED Operation: Under Forward Bias (P connected to +, N to -), the external electric field cancels the internal field, narrowing the depletion region. Driven electrons and holes continuously cross the junction and recombine, releasing potential energy as photons ($E = hf$)!
