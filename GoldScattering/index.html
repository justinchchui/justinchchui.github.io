Experimental Design

A radioactive Radium source emitting positively charged alpha particles (He²⁺) was housed inside a heavy lead cavity with a narrow aperture. This produced a focused, straight beam aimed at an ultra-thin gold foil sheet (~400 nm thick, about 1000 atoms thick).

Thomson vs Rutherford

In Thomson's Plum Pudding Model, positive charge was uniformly spread throughout the atom with negatively charged electrons embedded inside. Positively charged alpha particles are attracted to electrons and repelled by the positive sphere, resulting in nearly zero net deflection (< 0.1°). Rutherford's Nuclear Model proved positive charge (+79e in gold) is concentrated in a tiny, dense nucleus, producing strong localized Coulomb forces.

Coulomb Scattering Physics

Deflection angle θ depends on the impact parameter b (perpendicular distance of approach to nucleus). Small b results in massive electrostatic repulsion F = k·(2e)·(79e)/r², creating hyperbolic scattering orbits and backscattering.
